Introduction

Slides: Asia before Colonial Times

This lecture looks at where the languages of East and Southeast Asia came from and how they spread. Languages expand for several reasons: the geography and climate of the region; agriculture, in particular the domestication of crops and animals; trade by land and sea; and political and religious contact.

Contact between the languages of the region is ancient, and loanwords are the evidence for it. Because loanwords are often everyday words, they add to what history and archaeology tell us about the past.

Important concepts:

  • Language family
  • Domestication
  • Agricultural and technological expansion
  • Hunters and gatherers
  • Loan words
  • Language contact
