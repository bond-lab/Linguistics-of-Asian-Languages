    \item Endangered
    \item In contact with lingua franca Alor Malay
    \item APV word order (agent-patient-verb $\approx$ SOV)
    \item Lots of verbal morphology

\frametitle{abui}
\begin{itemize}
\item P-indexing is differential and complex!
\item Abui transitive verbs can index P with a prefix (not A)
\item Indexing can be conditioned by various factors
\begin{itemize}
\item Lexical verb
\item Animacy
\item Affectedness, Volition, Definiteness, Specificity
\end{itemize}
\begin{itemize}

\item Some verbs alternate; others do not
\end{itemize}
\item There are 8 prefix/proclitic paradigms or zero-indexing
\item Focus on 5 most frequent 3rd person forms
\end{itemize}
