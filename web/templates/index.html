Overview

Francis (フランシス) Bond (凡土) : 2024, 2025, 2026 (DAS 5LAJ/6LAJ)

Thursday 15:45-18:15, Room 2.42 tř. Svobody 26, 779 00 Olomouc

This course is an introduction to the linguistics of East and Southeast Asian languages. The course has the following objectives:

  • The course introduces the largest language groups and the context in which they are spoken and introduces students to basic literature.
  • Part of the course will be work with metaphors in languages of the region.

After completing the course, students will be able to distinguish and compare the language groups of the area, and to describe the current relationships between them, and between official and regional or tribal languages. Each student studies one grammatical or linguistic phenomenon in a language or languages of the area and writes a seminar paper about it, showing both knowledge of the specialist literature and the ability to analyse and describe the phenomenon.

Seminar work can be developed individually or in groups so that it is clear which part each student is responsible for. In the last week, students prepare a short informative presentation about their work for their colleagues.

Assessment

The prerequisites for successful completion are the following (details and milestones on the assessment page):

  • Regular attendance and active participation in class
  • Random notes (6 observations of things related to the course topic that surprised you in the discussion forum)
  • Checking at least 30 rows of the class metaphor data (not graded)
  • Presentation on a minority language or metaphors (week 10)
  • Presentation on the chosen topic (final week)
  • Seminar paper on the chosen topic (range 5-10 pages)
